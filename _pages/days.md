# BiGre Days: December 7th, 2026 @ IMAG

Join us for this second edition of the BiGre Day, a one-day conference bringing together the vibrant community of computational biology, biostatistics, bioinformatics, and biophysics in Grenoble.

We aim to provide a platform for young (and less young) local researchers to share their work and foster collaboration.

The event will take place in the [IMAG](https://batiment.imag.fr/public/plan/) building's main amphitheater.

## Registration, abstract submission, and other information

- **Conference date**: December 7th 2026 (more precise schedule TBA)

- **Registration** is free but mandatory, [using this form](https://framaforms.org/bigre-day-2026-1790948231-0)

- **Lunch and coffee breaks** will be provided

- **Deadline** for abstract submission for a contributed talk was November 9th 2026

- **Talk acceptance notifications** were sent on November 17th 2026
