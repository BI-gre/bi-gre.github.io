A non-exhaustive list of labs or teams who do some form of computational biology in Grenoble and participate to the BIGRE! seminars:

- [Tree-compbio](https://tree-timc.github.io/compbio): computational biology group in the TIMC (biomedical engineering) lab, within the Tree team
- [bioinfo@LIG](https://bioinfo-lig.github.io/): bioinformatics group in the LIG (computer science lab), within the Aptikal team
- [SVH](https://svh.imag.fr/pmwiki.php) team in LJK (applied mathematics lab)
- [LECA](https://leca.osug.fr/): alpine ecology lab
- [IAB](https://iab-grenoble.fr/): institute for advanced biosciences
- [Microcosme](https://team.inria.fr/microcosme) team at INRIA / LiPhy (interdisciplinary laboratory of physics)
- [Biosanté](https://biosante-lab.fr/en): Biology and biotechnologies for health laboratory
- [BGE](https://www.bge-lab.fr/en): Biosciences and bioengineering for health laboratory
